# LocalPulse Logo & Favicon Guidelines

## Logo Variants
- **Full Logo (Horizontal)**: Primary version for headers and navigation.
- **Stacked Logo**: For centered layouts, marketing materials, and social profiles.
- **Icon-only**: Used as the app icon and favicon.

## Visual Identity
- **Atmosphere**: Calm, neighborly, trustworthy, and organic.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Sage Green (#6B8F71): Primary trust and community signal.
  - Charcoal (#2E2B27): Grounding text and structural elements.
- **Typography**: Clean modern sans-serif (Inter/Be Vietnam Pro) with generous letter spacing.

## Clear Space & Sizing
- **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum space equal to 50% of the symbol's height around all logo variants.
- **Minimum Size**:
  - Horizontal: 120px width
  - Stacked: 80px width
  - Icon: 16px (favicon)

## Favicon Set
- 16x16: Simplified dot/pulse hint.
- 32x32: Clear house-pulse silhouette.
- 48x48: Detailed icon-only version.
